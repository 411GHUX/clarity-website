Check In Before You Begin

Before Clarity asks you to think, it asks how you're doing; your body, your head and your surroundings. If you're running on empty, it says so, gently, and lets you decide whether to keep going. Nothing gets locked behind it.

Privacy Policy

Last updated September 2026

Clarity is still in development. This policy describes our current plan, and will be finalized before launch.

Clarity will require an account for sign-in, billing, and keeping your data organized across sessions. We're designing the app so that the content of your decisions (what you enter, where you land) stays stored on your device rather than on our servers. Account information, like your email and subscription status, will be the only part that lives in our systems.

By default, your decisions are also excluded from your phone's standard iCloud backup, so they exist only on the device itself. If you'd like the extra safety net of including them in your own iCloud backup, you can turn that on in Settings. It's your choice, and it stays off unless you turn it on. You can also export any individual decision on your own terms, as a PDF, through AirDrop, or saved to Files, any time you want a personal copy outside the app.

What we protect, and what's yours to protect

Clarity is responsible for protecting the account information stored in our systems, such as your email, sign-in credentials, and billing details, using industry standard security practices. Because the content of your decisions is designed to stay on your device rather than on our servers, its protection depends on your device's own security, and on your iCloud account's security if you've chosen to include your decisions in your backup. We can't protect information stored on your device, or in your own iCloud backup, from someone who gains access to it. This includes someone who's able to unlock your phone or sign into your account. We'd encourage protecting your device with a passcode or biometric lock and keeping your sign-in details private, particularly if what you're working through in Clarity is sensitive to you.

Once accounts are live, this policy will be updated with full detail on what's stored, where, and how to delete it, including a real, working "delete my account" option, not just a settings toggle.
